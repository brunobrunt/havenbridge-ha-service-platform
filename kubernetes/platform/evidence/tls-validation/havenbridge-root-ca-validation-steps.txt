HavenBridge TLS Phase — Root CA Validation Steps
================================================

Purpose
-------
Validate that the HavenBridge Root CA certificate was issued successfully and
that cert-manager created the backing TLS Secret.

Recommended evidence directory
------------------------------
/home/alabi/projects/havenbridge-ha-service-platform/kubernetes/platform/evidence/tls-validation/

1. Validate the Certificate
---------------------------
kubectl get certificate havenbridge-root-ca   --namespace cert-manager   --output wide

Expected:
- READY=True
- SECRET=havenbridge-root-ca
- ISSUER=havenbridge-selfsigned

2. Validate the Secret
----------------------
kubectl get secret havenbridge-root-ca   --namespace cert-manager   --output wide

Expected:
- TYPE=kubernetes.io/tls
- Secret exists in cert-manager namespace
